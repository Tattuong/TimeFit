Privacy Policy – TimeFit

TimeFit is a private, on-device planner. Your tasks, plans, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload task text.

Data stored on the device

  • Tasks, plans, and completion stats
  • Coin balance and shop unlocks
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and TimeFit Pro

Services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and TimeFit Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N294.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
