# Google Play listing — TimeFit

Package: `com.TimeFitMNG.TimeFit`

## Title

TimeFit

## Short description

Fit the right tasks into the free time you have.

## Full description

TimeFit answers “I have 45 minutes — what should I do?” Each task has a duration, an optional due date, and a focus level. Enter a free window and the app builds a short plan, splits a long task into a smaller session, and lets you drag the order. Replan when the day changes.

Looks and TimeFit Pro (more than 8 open tasks) unlock with coins from finishing a task, the daily reward, or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and TimeFit Pro are one-time unlocks.
